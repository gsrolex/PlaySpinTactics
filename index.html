A roguelike strategy game inside a slot machine.

Pick your Companions and the order they fire in, change your symbols with Gems, and spend each Action on a spin or a swap. The reels hand you the pieces; the plan is yours.

No real-money gambling, no microtransactions.

Equip eight from 90+ Companions. Within each timing they fire in slot order, left to right, and many boost the one next to them, so where you put them matters.

The same eight in a different order make a different engine. You can't hold two copies of one Companion, so a duplicate ranks it up instead.

Every encounter gives you a score goal and a few Actions. The reels hand you the symbols, and you build the engine that scores them.

A spin deals a fresh board and triggers your Live symbols. Your combos score when you end the round. Eight encounters make a run.

Gems change a symbol for the rest of the run: its colour, when it scores, how much it scores, or turn it into a copy of another.

A symbol you fix in round two is still scoring in round nine.

SPIN

Every spin and every swap costs an Action. A spin deals a completely fresh board, so whatever you had set up is gone.

OR
SWAP

With your last Action it's your call: spin for a new board, or swap to finish the combo in front of you.

Connect three or more of one colour for a combo, from SMALL up to GALACTIC. Level up mid-round to pick a new Companion, and sell anything you don't need into the Black Hole.

Then the bosses push back, and each one changes how you have to play.

The Hourglass

One round only, with 9 Actions.

Runs earn credits. Unlock new Companions from Discovery Packs and build the Portal you start with from Chips.

Beat the game and Ascension makes every boss ask for more, for as long as you keep winning.
