Overview

Slot machines meet tactical strategy

Every symbol on your reels is known. When they land is not. Spin Tactics turns the slot machine into a game of decisions: connect three or more symbols of one colour to chain combos, recruit Companions that fire in order, and socket Gems to bend every spin in your favour.

Spin

Actions are scarce. Every spin and every swap costs one, so each pull of the lever is a real decision, not a reflex.

Push

Choose when to stop spinning and bank what you built. Every spin pays your Live symbols, but deals a fresh board and wipes your combo.

Outsmart

Stack Companion synergies, socket Gems, and sacrifice to the Black Hole until luck isn't part of the plan anymore.

Features

What's inside

Tactical Slot Strategy

Each round gives you a set number of Actions to reach a score goal. Position symbols, trigger chains, and turn a random board into an engine.

Jackpots & Route Choice

Jackpots and boss challenges keep encounters varied, and you choose your route between them.

100+ Companions

Equip eight and keep four in your bag. They fire in order, so where you place them decides how your engine runs.

Gems That Stay All Run

Change a symbol's colour or timing, clone it, or make it trigger instantly. Gems stay for the whole run.

The Black Hole

Sacrifice Companions, symbols or Gems for rewards. A well-timed sacrifice can save a run, or become the whole build.

Ascension & Leaderboards

Runs take 30 to 60 minutes. Clear the game and Ascension raises the challenge with tougher bosses, and every run counts on the online leaderboards.
