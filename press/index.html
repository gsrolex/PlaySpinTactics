About the game

Spin Tactics is a tactical roguelike where you rig the slot machine. Every spin fills the board, and every Action counts.

Connect three or more symbols of one colour and watch your combos climb from SMALL all the way to GALACTIC. Build your engine from Companions that fire in order, so where you place them matters. Socket Gems that change a symbol's colour or timing, or clone it, and keep them for the whole run. Sacrifice Companions, symbols or Gems to the Black Hole for rewards. Level up mid-round for new Companions, and take on bosses that fight back by draining your score or taking away spins.

Between runs, spend credits on booster packs of Chips and Portals to build your own setup. Clear the game and Ascension raises the challenge, with tougher bosses every level. Every run counts on the online leaderboards.

No real-money gambling. No microtransactions.

Features

  • Rig the machine. A slot machine where you make the decisions: swap, stamp, clone and recolour symbols to set up the combo you want.
  • Companions that fire in order. Over 100 Companions. Equip eight and keep four in your bag. Placement and timing (Live, On Spin, Resolve, Passive) decide how your engine runs.
  • Gems that stay all run. Change a symbol's colour or timing, clone it, or make it trigger instantly.
  • The Black Hole. Sacrifice Companions, symbols or Gems for rewards.
  • Level ups and bosses. Level up mid-round for new Companions. Bosses fight back by draining your score or taking away spins.
  • Ascension and leaderboards. Tougher bosses every level, and every run counts online.

About the developer

Hi, I'm Ammon, and Dead Broke Games is just me. I've been making Spin Tactics on my own for almost two years, mostly in the evenings and late at night. I always liked the feeling of a slot machine, but I wanted to be the one making the decisions, so I made the game I wanted to play. I'm really proud of how it turned out, and I hope you enjoy it.
